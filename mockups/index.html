Jana Apartman — look & feel

Five self-contained design mockups for the apartment homepage in Balatonkeresztúr. Each opens as a full static page (no build, works offline), with a HU/EN/DE language switch and a placeholder availability calendar. Pick one to develop into the real site.

What's inside each mockup

  • Sections: hero · about · cycling & e-bike charging · amenities · gallery · availability + booking · location · contact
  • Language switch (HU / EN / DE) powered by assets/js/i18n.js
  • Placeholder month calendar from assets/js/availability.js — later swapped for live Google Calendar free/busy
  • Images are gradient placeholders until real photos arrive
